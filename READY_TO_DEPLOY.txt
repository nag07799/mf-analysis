IMPORTANT NOTES
==============

⚠️  Free Tier Specifics:
  • Render services spin down after 15 min of inactivity
  • First request after idle takes ~30-60 seconds (cold start)
  • This is NORMAL and expected for free tier
  • To keep service warm, add a health check curl in cron

⚠️  Database Storage:
  • Free PostgreSQL: 100 MB limit
  • Current data: ~5-10 MB
  • 12+ months of growth: ~50-80 MB
  • If hitting limit: upgrade Render ($7/mo)

⚠️  First Deployment:
