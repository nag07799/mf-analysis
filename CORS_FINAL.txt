UPDATE CORS ON RENDER
====================

Go to: Render Dashboard → mf-api Web Service → Environment

Find: CORS_ORIGINS

Replace the value with:

["https://mf-analysis-equity-git-main-mutual-fund1.vercel.app","http://localhost:3000"]

Then click "Save Changes"

Wait 2-3 minutes for the service to redeploy.

Done! Your app will then work! ✅
